## Section 2: Investigative Journalism / Major Reports

### 11. Trapped: Neglected Elevators Put Chicago's Public Housing Residents At Risk
- **URL:** https://projects.bettergov.org/2018/trapped/neglected-elevators/
- **Also at:** https://interactive.wbez.org/elevators/
- **Publication:** Better Government Association / WBEZ (joint investigation)
- **Date:** June 4, 2018
- **Authors:** Alejandra Cancino et al.
- **Summary:** A seven-month investigation analyzing thousands of public records found that in 2015, firefighters responded to elevator rescues at 54 of 88 CHA buildings (nearly two-thirds)--a rate more than four times higher than other Chicago buildings. None of the CHA's 150 elevators was inspected for the entire year of 2016. In the prior year, 136 elevators flunked inspections yet were still issued operating certificates. Despite $480 million in modernization spending, only cosmetic elevator improvements were made.

## Section 4: Legal Resources and Tenant Rights Analysis

### 18. Chicago RLTO 5-12-110 Tenant Remedies (full text)
- **URL:** https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/chicago/latest/chicago_il/0-0-0-2639177
- **Publication:** American Legal Publishing (official Chicago Municipal Code)
- **Date:** Current / ongoing
- **Summary:** The official text of the RLTO tenant remedies section. Key provisions: after 14 days' written notice, tenants may withhold rent reflecting the reduced value of the premises, deduct repair costs, or terminate the lease. Failure to maintain elevators per municipal code is a listed violation triggering these remedies. Withholding is capped at $500 or half the monthly rent, whichever is greater.

### 19. Chicago Tenants' Rights Guide 2025 | RLTO Law Explained
- **URL:** https://bc-firm.com/tenants-rights-chicago/
- **Publication:** Brabender Law LLC (bc-firm.com)
- **Date:** 2025
- **Summary:** Comprehensive guide to Chicago tenant rights under the RLTO. Lists broken elevators as a common violation of landlord maintenance duties. Explains that landlords must maintain the entire property including common areas, and failure to maintain elevators triggers tenant remedies including rent withholding and lease termination.

### 20. Chicago Landlord Tenant Ordinance - Know Your Rights

## Key Legal Takeaways for Chicago Tenants with Elevator Outages

1. **Elevators are part of the "premises"** under the RLTO -- loss of elevator service = loss of use of a portion of the premises.
2. **14-day written notice required** before exercising remedies (rent withholding, repair-and-deduct, or lease termination).
3. **Rent withholding** is capped at $500 or half the monthly rent, whichever is greater.
4. **Lease termination** is available if the problem persists for 72+ hours after notice (for conditions affecting health/safety).
5. **311 complaints** create a paper trail and trigger city inspections; landlords get 15 days from citation to fix the problem.
6. **Free legal help** is available through Law Center for Better Housing (lcbh.org) and Uptown People's Law Center (773-769-1411).
